🔐 What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, smartphones, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

Cybersecurity is important because we use technology every day for banking, education, communication, shopping, business and entertainment.

💡 Beginner Tip:

You do not need to become an expert hacker to understand cybersecurity. Start by learning how attacks happen and how to protect yourself.

🛡️ 10 Cybersecurity Safety Tips

1️⃣ Use strong passwords

Use long and unique passwords for important accounts.

2️⃣ Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever it is available.

3️⃣ Avoid suspicious links

Do not click links from unknown or suspicious messages.

4️⃣ Update your software

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

5️⃣ Use secure Wi-Fi

Avoid entering sensitive information on unknown public Wi-Fi.

6️⃣ Protect your phone

Use a screen lock and keep your phone software updated.

7️⃣ Watch for phishing

Check the sender and website address before providing information.

8️⃣ Backup important files

Keep backups of important documents and personal files.

9️⃣ Install trusted software

Download applications only from trusted sources.

🔟 Protect personal information

Avoid unnecessarily sharing sensitive information online.

📝 Practical Cybersecurity Exercise

Scenario

You receive an email saying that your bank account will be suspended unless you click a link and enter your username, password and OTP.

Question

What should you do?

  1. Do not click the link.
  2. Do not provide your password or OTP.
  3. Check the official bank application or website.
  4. Contact the bank using an official contact method.
  5. Report the suspicious message if appropriate.
