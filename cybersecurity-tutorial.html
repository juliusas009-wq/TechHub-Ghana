What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, smartphones, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage or theft.

We use technology every day for banking, education, communication, shopping, business and entertainment.

Understanding basic cybersecurity helps you recognize threats and protect your devices, accounts and personal information.

💡 Beginner Tip

You do not need to become a professional cybersecurity expert before you can protect yourself. Start by learning how common attacks work and how to defend against them.

🛡️ 10 Cybersecurity Safety Tips

1️⃣ Use strong passwords

Use long and unique passwords for important accounts.

2️⃣ Enable 2FA

Enable two-factor authentication whenever it is available.

3️⃣ Avoid suspicious links

Do not click links from unknown or suspicious messages.

4️⃣ Update software

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated.

5️⃣ Use secure Wi-Fi

Avoid entering sensitive information on untrusted networks.

6️⃣ Protect your phone

Use a strong screen lock and keep your phone software updated.

7️⃣ Watch for phishing

Check senders and website addresses before providing information.

8️⃣ Backup important files

Keep backups of important documents and personal files.

9️⃣ Install trusted software

Download applications from trusted sources.

🔟 Protect personal information

Avoid unnecessarily sharing sensitive information online.

📝 Practical Cybersecurity Exercise

Scenario

You receive a message saying that your bank account will be suspended unless you click a link and enter your username, password and OTP.

What should you do?

  1. Do not click the suspicious link.
  2. Do not provide your password or OTP.
  3. Check the official banking application or website.
  4. Contact the bank through an official contact method.
  5. Report the suspicious message when appropriate.
