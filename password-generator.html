🛡️ Password Security Tips

  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Prefer long passwords rather than short, predictable passwords.
  • Avoid using names, birthdays, phone numbers or other easily guessed information.
  • Consider using a reputable password manager to store your passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication whenever an account supports it.
  • Never share your passwords with other people.
